Origin & Mission

Bridging the Gap in Financial Literacy

Designed specifically to transform complex financial theories into intuitive, hands-on knowledge for high school and university students.

The Reality

The Literacy Gap

Only 11% of 15-year-old students achieve high proficiency in financial literacy globally[cite: 3]. Most traditional resources leave beginners overwhelmed with complex jargon and abstract equations without clear practical context.

Our Purpose

Finance for BIT

We provide a structured, student-centric pathway that strips away confusion[cite: 3]. By combining first-principles learning with interactive tools, we prepare students for both academic excellence and real-world finance careers[cite: 3].

Our Curriculum Pillars

A step-by-step pathway from foundational concepts to institutional-grade valuation[cite: 3].

01

Financial Accounting

Master the universal language of business through standardized financial statements, cash flow linkages, and balance sheet mechanics[cite: 3].

02

Corporate Finance

Understand how corporations make decisions—covering Time Value of Money (TVM), capital budgeting, risk vs. return, and capital structure[cite: 3].

03

DCF Valuation

Learn the gold standard of company valuation by projecting free cash flows, determining WACC, and calculating intrinsic value[cite: 3].

04

Applied Finance

Translate theoretical finance into career pathways across Investment Banking, Equity Research, Private Equity, and Asset Management[cite: 3].

Built for Intuitive Learning
